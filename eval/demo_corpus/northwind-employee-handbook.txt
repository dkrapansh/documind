Northwind Traders Employee Handbook

Section 4: Paid Time Off

4.1 Vacation Accrual
Full-time employees accrue vacation at a rate of 1.25 days per month,
totaling 15 paid vacation days in their first year of continuous
employment. Vacation days accrue monthly and may be used once earned,
subject to manager approval for scheduling.

4.2 Vacation Increases With Tenure
After three years of continuous service, the annual vacation allotment
increases to 20 days per year. After five years, employees accrue 25
days per year. Vacation accrual rates are reviewed annually by Human
Resources and are subject to change with 90 days notice.

4.3 Sick Leave
Full-time employees receive 8 paid sick days per calendar year,
accrued at a rate of 0.67 days per month. Unused sick days do not
carry over to the following year.

Section 5: Remote Work Policy

5.1 Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period and
whose role does not require regular on-site presence may request a
remote work arrangement, subject to manager and HR approval.

5.2 Equipment
Northwind Traders provides a laptop, monitor, and standard peripherals
to approved remote employees. Employees are responsible for
maintaining a safe and functional home office environment.

Section 6: Code of Conduct

6.1 Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and vendors
with respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, and
retaliation of any kind are strictly prohibited.

6.2 Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company information, client
data, or trade secrets to any third party without written authorization
from a department head.
